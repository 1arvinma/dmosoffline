DMOS Table privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

DMOS Table is an open-source campaign tool. It stores campaigns, character records and settings locally on your device. The app does not require an account, include advertising or analytics, or send campaign data to the developer. It has no Android Internet permission.

Your files and sharing

You choose files to import. You can export backups and player sheets, print documents or choose an app from your device's share menu. Exported files are not encrypted by DMOS. A selected file provider, printer or recipient app handles your data under its own policies. Windows sheet sharing copies text to the system clipboard, where other applications with clipboard access may read it.

Device music

Music controls send standard media-button commands to the active player and adjust music volume. DMOS does not read your music library, notifications, account or listening history.

Storage and deletion

Campaigns remain in private local app storage until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the Android app. Windows uninstall retains the local profile. Exports and shared copies remain wherever you saved or sent them and must be deleted separately. Local recovery snapshots may contain previous campaign data. No server-side account or developer-held campaign database exists.

Support

If you contact support, your email or messaging provider transmits the message and any attachments you choose. Share only information you intend to send. Use the project GitHub issue tracker for support; do not post private data.

Changes

A future release that changes these practices will update this policy.
